# Base Converter Privacy Policy

English | [中文](#中文)

Effective date: June 11, 2026

## English

Base Converter is a utility app for converting decimal, hexadecimal, and binary number representations. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the app.

### Information We Do Not Collect

Base Converter does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide personal information.

The app's core conversion features run on your device. The numbers you enter for conversion are processed locally and are not sent to our server by the app.

### Advertising

Base Converter may display ads provided by Google AdMob.

Google AdMob and related Google services may collect or process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, approximate location, app interactions, diagnostics, and other data used to provide, measure, personalize, or improve ads, depending on your device settings, consent choices, region, and Google's policies.

You can learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services here:

https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can manage ad personalization through your iOS device settings and Google account settings where available.

### Analytics

Base Converter does not include a separate analytics service operated by us. However, advertising SDKs may provide aggregated ad performance, diagnostics, or measurement information.

### Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We do not operate our own user account system or user database for Base Converter.

Information related to ads may be processed by Google AdMob and its service providers according to their own policies and applicable laws.

### Children

Base Converter is a general utility app and is not directed specifically to children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through any support channel, please contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

### Data Security

The app is designed to keep conversion input local to your device. No method of electronic processing or transmission is completely secure, but we aim to minimize data handling by keeping the core utility features local.

### Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If changes are made, the updated policy will be posted with a new effective date.

### Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer through the support channel listed on the App Store or the support page for this app.
